- **Trend 2: Lower-Tier Cities Become OPC Entrepreneurship Depression**: While entrepreneurs in first-tier cities are discussing AI ethics, entrepreneurs in lower-tier cities have already doubled their income with AI. - **Trend 3: Standardization of Human-Computer Collaboration SOP**: More and more OPC entrepreneurs will precipitate replicable human-computer collaboration processes, such as the standardized SOP of “human set goals - AI produce plans - human screening - AI execution”, further reducing the entrepreneurship threshold.
- **Trend 4: Mature OPC Service Ecosystem**: There will be specialized registration, fiscal and tax, and legal services for OPC, further reducing the institutional cost of single-person entrepreneurship.

## 6. Practical Suggestions for OPC Entrepreneurs

> ⚠️ Precautions
>
> - Don’t make “showy technology” AI, make “utility” AI: users can’t feel its existence, but they can’t live without it when they leave. Such products have long-term value.
> - Run through the minimum closed loop before scaling up: don’t invest a lot of costs at the beginning, first make a minimum viable product (MVP), and scale up after verifying that there are paying users.
> - Attach importance to professional knowledge in vertical fields: AI is a tool, your understanding of the industry is the barrier, don’t blindly follow the trend to make general tools.
> - Do a good job in risk control: although OPC has low cost, a single person bears all risks. It is recommended to do a good job in fiscal and tax compliance and contract specifications in advance to avoid legal disputes.
