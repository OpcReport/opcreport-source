## 🎯 Hot Tracks for OPC Entrepreneurs

### Track 1: AI Video — First-Mover Advantage

A Shenzhen team produced 23 episodes of AI anime series using Jimeng AI (storyboards) and Jianying AI (synthesis) for a total cost of only **RMB 12,000**. In one month, the series achieved **100 million+ views** and **RMB 400,000+** in platform revenue — a 1:33 ROI. The key to AI video is not quality alone but **speed**: discovering new formats one week before the crowd.

### Track 2: Niche Micro-Accounts

General content is saturated, but **vertical niches remain wide open**. Accounts like “AI teaches seniors health exercises” outperform generic AI education accounts for monetization. The 2026 keyword is **vertical, vertical, vertical**.

### Track 3: AI Agent Platforms & Development

The 2026 AI Agent landscape shows a **“duopoly + niche supplement”** pattern: LangChain/LangGraph dominates enterprise, OpenClaw leads personal productivity and SMB. Internet giants (Alibaba Bailian, ByteDance Coze, Tencent Yuanqi) are pivoting from consumer products to government/enterprise markets.

### Track 4: Traditional Industry AI Retrofit

From solar to steel, from apparel to lighting — traditional industries are being **AI-retrofitted into new money printers**. A Qingdao tech company runs full AI enablement with just 4 core team members. Every traditional business owner should ask: *Can my old business get an AI engine?*

### Track 5: Cross-Border E-Commerce & Overseas Services

AI Listing factories generate **trilingual listings in 30 seconds** with A9 algorithm optimization. OPC solopreneurs can now run full-stack operations on Amazon, TikTok Shop, and other global platforms.

### Track 6: Prompt Engineering & AI Skill Marketplace

“Knowing how to use AI” means “knowing how to command AI.” Prompt engineers have become an emerging profession. The **OPC Agent Skill Marketplace** (yzt.yess.net.cn) is now live, facilitating AI skill product trading and monetization.

### Trend 2: Multimodal AI Goes Fully Practical

Multimodal LLMs break text/image/audio boundaries. Midjourney, Sora, Runway give solopreneurs professional-grade content production. AI evolves from **passive response** to **active decision-making**.
