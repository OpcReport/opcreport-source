## 💡 OPC Entrepreneurship Playbook: Three Proven Paths

### Path 1: Vertical Domain Agent (Highest Potential)

Build bespoke automation workflows for specific professional audiences (lawyers, HR, designers).

- **LegalBrief.ai:** Lawyers upload PDF contracts → AI flags risky clauses + generates revision suggestions (Llama 3 + RAG + regulatory vector DB)
- **HireFlow:** HR inputs JD → AI screens resumes, generates interview questions, scores candidates
- **Monetization:** $19/month or $199/year subscriptions
- **Validation time:** 3-5 days (Vercel AI SDK + LangChain)

### Path 2: SaaS Plugin Market Arbitrage

Build AI feature add-ons for platforms with existing user bases and payment rails (Notion, Figma, Linear). Zero cold-start traffic problem.

### Path 3: AI-Native Content Creation

The “cost collapse” of content production via AI is restructuring the entire content industry. Dragon Bone Animation (one-person, text-to-2D-animation) and AI Sleep Companion are exemplars.

> **⚠️ Avoid the Trap:** General-purpose AI assistants are dominated by major players with data flywheels and ecosystem lock-in. Focus on **narrow, workflow-embedded vertical agents** where domain expertise matters more than raw model capability.
