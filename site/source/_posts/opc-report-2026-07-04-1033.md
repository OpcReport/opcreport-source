## ⚠️ Engineering Traps and Survival Guide

### 🚨 Traps to Avoid

**Q1: Is the general-purpose assistant track suitable for individuals or small teams?**

**A1:** Absolutely not. The general-purpose assistant track has been monopolized by leading giants with a closed loop of advantages. The triple advantages of computing power clusters, data flywheels, and ecosystem barriers are definitely not something individuals or small teams can break through in the short term. Entering means becoming a “companion runner.”

**Q2: What type of Agent entrepreneurship is suitable for individuals or small teams?**

**A2:** Focus on segmented AI Agents with “vertical scenarios + strong workflow embedding”, especially those adapting to narrow fields requiring the dual capabilities of “industry Know-How (professional experience) + non-standard data processing.”

### 💡 Survival Strategies

- **Narrow and Deep > Wide and Shallow:** Choose a niche field and excel to the extreme
- **Rapid Validation > Perfect Product:** Use MVPs to test the market, instead of working behind closed doors
- **Revenue First > Funding First:** Generate cash flow as early as possible, instead of burning money for growth
- **Systems Thinking > Freelance Mindset:** Build reusable products/services, instead of selling time
- **Community-Driven > Ad-Driven:** Attract users through real value, instead of paying for traffic
