- **Capital Attention:** VCs beginning to focus on “light-asset, high-margin” OPC projects; first OPC-specific funds may appear

### 4.2 Three Recommendations for OPC Entrepreneurs

#### ✅ Recommendation 1: Find a “Niche Track,” Don’t Think About “Disrupting the Industry”

OPC’s advantage is “small boat, easy to turn.” Choose a specific, segmented, pain point with willingness to pay, and go deep. For example, “providing code review tools for indie developers” is easier to succeed than “building a general AI assistant.”

#### ✅ Recommendation 2: “Automate” Before “Scale”

Many OPCs die from “one person doing three people’s work.” The correct path: first use AI tools to automate repetitive work (customer service, formatting, data organization), freeing up time for core value (product iteration, user communication).

#### ✅ Recommendation 3: Build an “External Brain,” Don’t Store Everything in Your Own Head

The biggest risk of OPC is the “founder bottleneck” - all decisions and knowledge reside in one person’s brain. Recommended: Use Notion/Obsidian + AI to crystallize processes, decision logic, and user feedback into “inheritable assets.”
