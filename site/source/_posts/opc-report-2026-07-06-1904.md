## 📈 Three Major OPC Trends in 2026

### Trend 1: Extreme Vertical Specialization

Super individuals no longer pursue broad markets but lock onto specific niche markets, improving product and service quality through AI. Examples include elderly health management, intelligent contract review, vertical AI applications. This focus increases success rates and professionalizes the market.

### Trend 2: Strong Collaboration

Different from traditional solo entrepreneurship, modern “super individuals” emphasize collaboration. Building ecosystems together creates resource sharing. AI tech companies partner with traditional industry entrepreneurs to empower digital transformation.

### Trend 3: Zero-Cost Entrepreneurship Environment

Using open-source tools, cloud platforms, and social media promotion, they achieve transformation from nothing. DeepSeek and other open-source models have lowered AI costs significantly. Zero-cost entrepreneurship enables more people to participate in the entrepreneurial wave.
