### ⚡ Engineering Pitfalls for Individual AI Agent Startups

> **Unsuitable Track:** The general assistant track has been monopolized by leading giants. The triple advantage of computing clusters, data flywheels, and ecosystem barriers cannot be overcome by individuals or small teams in the short term.

### Correct Approach for Individual Agent Entrepreneurship

- **Focus on Vertical Scenarios:** Choose narrow fields requiring both “industry Know-How + non-standard data processing”
