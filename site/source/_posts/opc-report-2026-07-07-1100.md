## ⚠️ Risk Warnings and Considerations

**⚡ Core Warning:** One-person company does NOT equal one person doing everything. In reality, solo entrepreneurs still need:

- Office space and computing resources
- Business registration, tax, and legal compliance
- Project connections and market feedback
- Industry exchange and supply chain collaboration

**OPC is essentially:**

- One person running a small but beautiful individual business
- Using AI tools and external collaboration to complete products, content, services, and market validation
- Creative and technical capabilities as core entry standards
- Independently completing product design, promotion, and commercial monetization
