## 📌 Breaking News: China’s First OPC Standard Released

**July 9, 2026** — The “Artificial Intelligence OPC Terminology” group standard was officially released, providing the first clear definition for one-person AI companies:

### Key Definitions

- **OPC (One Person Company)**: A company led and controlled by **one core natural person**, with generally **no more than 10 employees**
- **AI OPC**: Companies with AI technology research, development, application, or services as their main business
- **Core Asset**: The founder’s personal brand and professional capabilities
- **Operating Model**: Independent operation and scalable delivery through intelligent agents

### Standard Evolution

The definition tightened from “no more than 3 shareholders, no more than 20 employees” to “**1 person leading, no more than 10 people**,” signaling a push toward true solo entrepreneurship with AI amplification.
