## 🎯 Action Guide for Entrepreneurs

### Three Core Elements of OPC Success

1. **Choose the Right Track**: Content marketing, AI tool development, intelligent agent services, and creative industries are current hot directions
2. **Master AI Tools**: Integrate AI deeply into product development, marketing, and customer service
3. **Focus on Demand**: OPC’s core is “capturing a demand you can satisfy alone”—not pursuing comprehensive solutions

### ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Many people make a fundamental mistake: assuming OPC requires mastering AI and controlling multiple AI Agents. This often leads to failure because they misunderstand OPC’s core—it should be **demand-oriented, not technology-oriented**.
