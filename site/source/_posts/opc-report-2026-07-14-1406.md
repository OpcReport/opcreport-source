**Warning from Kai-Fu Lee (01.AI Founder):** Over the past two years, enterprise AI has clustered entirely in the execution layer, falling into a serious “efficiency trap.” Simply improving operational efficiency doesn’t change a company’s fundamental business metrics. **Translation for Solo Founders:** Don’t just use AI to do the same things faster. Use AI to do things differently—make better decisions, identify new opportunities, create novel value.
