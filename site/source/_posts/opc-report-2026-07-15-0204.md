## 6. Risks & Pitfalls

⚠️ **Key warnings:**

- **Cognitive trap:** Believing OPC requires mastering AI and stacking N agents to win — wrong. The core of OPC is a company that captures a need and gets paid for it.
- **Compliance cost:** A one-person LLC faces stricter financial supervision — annual financial reports must be audited by a CPA firm; company and personal assets must be strictly separated.
- **Agent security:** Prompt injection and excessive permissions enabling lateral movement are new runtime-security battlegrounds; build permission-aware retrieval, controlled writes, and human-in-the-loop guardrails.
- **Homogeneous competition:** Grand narratives + generic tools are already commoditized; small, personalized, passion-driven niches are easier to win.
