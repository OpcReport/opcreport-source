## ⚠️ Risk Warnings: Pitfalls in the Wind

### 🔴 Risk 1: Severe Homogenization

AI tools lower barriers, which means competitive moats are equally lowered. When everyone uses the same AI toolkit for marketing, content, and customer service, “one-person company” may turn into “one-person Red Ocean.” Differentiated core competitiveness is crucial.

### 🔴 Risk 2: Platform Dependency

Many OPCs heavily depend on a handful of AI platforms (OpenAI, Claude, etc.). If APIs get expensive or services are interrupted, the entire business faces paralysis risk. **Strategy: multi-platform, multi-tool combinations.**

### 🟡 Risk 3: Regulatory Compliance Window Closing

With OPC scale growth, data compliance, tax treatment, and IP ownership issues will gradually attract regulatory attention. The early-stage wild-growth window is narrowing.

### ✅ Recommended Strategies

- 🟢 Deeply cultivate vertical niche segments and build data/experience moats
- 🟢 Use multi-platform, multi-tool combinations to avoid single dependency
- 🟢 Establish standardized service processes early to improve replicability
- 🟢 Monitor policy developments — compliant operation is the foundation of long-term survival
