## Market & Trends

- **TrustMRR / 腾讯云 (Jul 16):** AI solopreneurs earning $10K+/month operate with an average team size of just 1.7 people; over 60% spend ≤$150/month on their tech stack. The game has changed — AI coding evolved from “AI writes code” to intent-driven systematic development, with solo developers matching small-team output.
- **企鹅号 (Jul 15):** AI Agent e-commerce operations are scaling from PPT concepts to real deployment. Unlike traditional chat-with-AI models, modern agents receive a goal, autonomously decompose steps, call tools, execute tasks, and verify results end-to-end.
- **CSDN / OpenCSG (Jul 2):** WAIC and OpenCSG launched the OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge — the world’s first top-tier incubator exclusively for super-individuals and AI entrepreneurs. Core formula: “Carbon-based wisdom (human) + Silicon-based execution (AI) = complete company.” One AI agent handles the daily task volume equivalent to 3-5 full-time employees at less than 1/10 the cost.
- **CSDN (Jul 1):** China’s national one-person LLC count exceeded 16 million as of June 2025. First-half 2025 registrations hit 2.86 million, up 47% YoY, accounting for 23.8% of all new businesses. AI LLMs and agents have become the “standard toolkit” for solo entrepreneurship.
