Yunnan province issued China’s first-ever dedicated policy for AI OPC (One Person Company) startups — a signal that national-level support for solo AI entrepreneurs is no longer theoretical.

**Policy & Regulation**

- **Yunnan HR Dept Issues China’s First OPC AI Startup Policy (9 Measures)** — Yunnan Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security published “Several Measures to Support AI OPC Entrepreneurship,” covering four pillars: carrier space, funding, talent, and services. Key benefits: free co-working desks, up to ¥5M credit loan for business legal entity, ¥5,000 one-time startup subsidy, and integrated ecosystem services with affordable compute. The province characterizes its OPC ecosystem as being in a “rapid scaling initiation phase.” (Qie Hao / Yunnan’s official release, Jul 16)
- **Incremental Policy Momentum for AI One-Person Companies** — Multiple cities and provinces are now explicitly endorsing OPC as a strategic growth category, with Yunnan joining Shenzhen, Shanghai, and Ningbo in offering dedicated support frameworks. (City Finance Net, Jul 15)
