### China / Mainland

- **Yunnan rolls out 9-point AI OPC support policy — corporate founders eligible for up to ¥30M in loans** (腾讯网, Jul 16) — Yunnan Human Resources and Social Security Department released dedicated measures covering workspace, financing, talent, and services. Corporate legal representatives can access up to ¥5M credit loan + ¥30M secured loan; individual founders up to ¥500K credit + ¥10M secured. One-time startup subsidy of ¥5,000; monthly internship subsidy up to ¥2,000 per hire. Skills training subsidy up to ¥7,280. Government commits to full lifecycle support via an OPC guidance expert database, and actively courts “super-individual” OPC founders domestically and internationally.
- **WAIC 2026 kicks off Jul 17–20 in Shanghai; solo-founder and AI super-individual theme central** (企鹅号, Jul 16) — World AI Conference 2026 runs Jul 17–20 in Shanghai with a new “WAIC City Walk 2026” program connecting 24 AI-themed venues across 6 districts. The conference highlights “一个人就是一支队伍” (one person = an entire team) as a core AI-era entrepreneurship narrative.
- **Chengdu tech island case study: 10-year veteran shifts from “manpower stacking” to “AI leverage”** (四川天府新区, Jul 15) — At Chengdu Xinglong Lake Science & Technology Ecology Island, brand strategy consultant Wu Qihuan operates his business with one laptop and multiple AI “employees.” The island ecosystem integrates computing power, green energy, data, and market channels into a one-stop incubation environment.
- **AI agent development training camp: Coze, Vibe Coding, ADK, LangChain, AutoGen in one curriculum** (企鹅号, Jul 16) — A practical training camp covering five mainstream tech stacks (Coze, Vibe Coding, ADK, LangChain, AutoGen) from zero foundation to advanced multi-agent collaborative development, targeting enterprise cost reduction and individual competitiveness.
- **2026 AI Agent tools comparison: Feishu aily, Real Agent, Baidu DuMate, OpenClaw** (企鹅号, Jul 16) — Feishu aily integrates natively with enterprise collaboration workflows; Real Agent (ZaiShi) connects to WeChat and DingTalk via TARS model + ISSUT screen semantic tech; Baidu DuMate leverages Wenxin large model for office scenarios; OpenClaw positions as a super-individual agent platform with multi-agent coordination.
- **2026 AI coding tools ranking: Trae leads, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Cline compete** (CSDN, Jul 13) — ByteDance’s AI-native IDE Trae became a phenomenon with its SOLO dual-agent mode (Builder + Coder) and completely free domestic version with Doubao-1.5-pro and DeepSeek R1/V3 models. GitHub Copilot maintains global commercial leadership; Cursor attracts indie developers with AI-first design.
- **Best AI agent platforms 2026: Mistral Vibe, June, Claude Cowork reviewed** (企鹅号, Jul 14) — Mistral Vibe connects 100+ tools via MCP protocol for deep research and overnight coding; June is macOS-exclusive open-source privacy-first agent combining chat, voice dictation, and meeting notes with zero data retention; Claude Cowork operates across files, apps, and plugins to deliver complete documents and reports.
