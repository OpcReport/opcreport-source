Solo founders now start roughly one-third of all new companies — a share that has climbed 53% in six years, according to multiple independent sources tracked this hour.

- **Tencent Cloud (Developer)** — 2026-07-16: A deep-dive reports the share of companies founded by solo founders rose from 23.7% in 2019 to 36.3% in 2025, a 53% jump. It argues 2026 is becoming the “year of AI Agent landing,” with one-person companies carrying vertical-disruption capability and zero organizational drag.
- **OpenCSG (CSDN / CNBlogs)** — 2026-07-14: Coverage of the “OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge” cites the same one-third figure and six-year 53% surge, framing the core formula as “carbon-based intelligence (human) + silicon-based execution (AI) = a complete company.”
- **Sohu Tech** — 2026-05-31: Roundup of scaled OPC wins — Sweden’s Lovable hit $100M ARR in 8 months at a $6.6B valuation; Israel’s Base44 (90% AI-generated code, 8 people) reached $1.5M ARR in 3 weeks before an $80M acquisition; Suzhou Huazong finished 2,600 AI-anime storyboards in 28 days.
- **China News Service / Penguin** — 2026-07-04: At the Global OPC Co-creation Festival, Beijing Future Tide founder Liu Yefei runs a company with 5 “AI employees” handling strategy, coding, code review, cloud deployment and research; his platform ships digital-employee kits for SMEs and expert avatars.
- **Penguin** — 2026-05-27: Zhima Enterprise Credit launched an “OPC toolbox” for one-person companies; Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Zhejiang and Jiangsu have rolled out support policies (free desks, subsidies, compute vouchers).
- **Penguin** — 2026-04-28: “Seven ways to run a one-person company” names the single-person short-drama content factory as a rising trend, with AI scripting the first role replaced from professional teams.
- **Tencent** — 2026-06-05: At its AI industry conference, Tencent Cloud’s Liu Yi framed “AI Agent second half: from super-individual to super-team,” launching WorkBuddy Enterprise and an office-agent suite.
- **GitHub · Solo Founders** — 2026-03-14: The Solo Founders Program (SFP) is a 3-month San Francisco residency with $100K investment and a 6-founder cohort (“If you’re already solo, you’re early”). Its “State of Solo Founding” report with Carta concludes solo founders now start 1/3 of all new companies.
- **Y Combinator · Lightcone Podcast** — 2026-06-23: Webflow co-founder Bryant Chou returns with Ploy, an AI website + marketing platform wired into analytics, CRM and search console. The episode argues domain-expert founders are making a comeback as AI lowers build cost.
- **Indie Hackers** — 2026-06-19: Fresh OPC field notes — a self-built cold-email tool (43 sent, 17 replies, 1 paid), IbexAI’s “Stop building the same product as others,” and a 3-week Stripe+auth+LLM-routing war story; the site added a Vibe Coding Tools section.
- **The Kevin Rose Show · a16z** — 2026-04-16: a16z’s Anish Acharya notes a personal-injury lawyer won a global Claude Code hackathon with software rivaling VC-backed startups, and predicts “SaaS is getting demolished” as agents let one person match a small team.
- **GitHub · rouor/one-person-company** — 2026-07-12: A curated OPC AI-tools repo (29 commits) benchmarks v0.dev, Lovable, Bolt.new and Replit on time, features and price for one-click site/app/game generation.
- **GitHub Topics · solopreneur** — 2026-03-27: 123 public repos now carry the solopreneur topic, including high-signal lists (real products, real revenue, real retros) and an open-source cross-provider AI dev team built on Claude Code + Codex CLI.
- **No-Code AI platforms (VirtusFlow / Botminds / Activepieces / Shnoco)** — 2026-07: The no-code + agentic-automation lane is heating up — VirtusFlow pushes zero-code BPA, Botminds targets lending/credit agents, Activepieces ships open-source automation (280+ integrations), and Shnoco curates 500+ tools for AI/no-code makers.
