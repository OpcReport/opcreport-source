Solo founders now account for 36.3% of all new companies — up 53% from 23.7% in 2019 — as AI agents mature from chat tools into “hands-on employees,” according to a Tencent Cloud analysis published today.

- **Tencent Cloud** (2026-07-16 20:05 UTC+8): New analysis reports that the share of companies founded by solo founders climbed from 23.7% in 2019 to 36.3% in 2025, a 53% jump in six years. It calls 2026 the “landing year” for AI agents, shifting from chatbots to workers that handle real tasks.
- **China News / Penguin** (2026-07-04): The Global OPC Co-Creation Festival gathered one-person-company founders across content marketing and music. A Beijing founder runs a startup with 5 “AI employees” covering planning, coding, review, deployment, and research.
- **Sohu** (2026-05-31): OPC scale-up cases surface — Sweden’s Lovable hit $100M annualized revenue in 8 months at a $6.6B valuation; Israel’s Base44 (8 people, 90% AI-generated code) reached $1.5M ARR in 3 weeks before an $80M acquisition.
- **Penguin** (2026-05-27): Zhima Business Credit launched an “OPC toolkit” for solo companies. Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Zhejiang and Jiangsu rolled out support policies — free desks, subsidies, compute vouchers.
- **CSDN / OpenCSG** (2026-07-14): OPC Independence Pioneer Challenge opens, echoing Sam Altman’s claim that “one person could build a $1B unicorn” in the AI era; over one-third of new companies are now solo-founded.
- **Tencent** (2026-02-08): 2026 framed as AI’s application-breakout year. A pure AI-agent social network, MoltBook, drew 100K+ agents and 10M+ spectators within 48 hours, with humans only watching.
- **IT168** (2026-06-05): Tencent Cloud launched WorkBuddy Enterprise and an office-agent suite at its AI industry conference, aiming to connect “super individuals” into AI-native “super teams.”
- **GitHub (rouor/one-person-company)** (2026-07-12): A continuously updated “one-person-company AI tools” repo covers AI chat, image/video, AI IDEs, smart browsers, and one-click website/App/game generation.
- **Indie Hackers** (2026-06-19): Solo MRR cases keep appearing — Jack Friks hit $35k MRR after three years of failure and learning to code; another maker reached $10k MRR by building an app off a physical-product audience.
- **TechCrunch** (2026-03-20): WordPress.com now lets AI agents draft, edit and publish posts, plus manage other site tasks — a move that could reshape how the web is maintained.
- **Indiemaker.co** (2026-07-15): A marketplace for buying and selling micro-startups launched, making indie projects “liquid assets” — from $1K experiments to $500K micro-companies.
- **PDFAssistant.ai** (2026-07-15): No-code AI PDF workflow automation built on the Adobe PDF Library — turn a conversation into a reusable, batch-runnable automation with zero code and end-to-end encryption.
- **The Kevin Rose Show (a16z’s Anish Acharya)** (2026-04-16): A personal-injury lawyer won a Claude Code hackathon with software rivaling VC-backed startups; Acharya predicts SaaS is being demolished and a four-day workweek is coming.
