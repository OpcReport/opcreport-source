The clearest signal this hour: the “one-person unicorn” is no longer theory. Medvi, a healthcare platform run by roughly two people, booked $400M in 2024 revenue and is guiding to $1.8B this year — while Alibaba reports 30–40% of its international merchants are effectively run by a single operator.

- **CSDN (Jul 10)** — Medvi cited as a flagship “one-person unicorn”: ~2-person team, $400M revenue in 2024, targeting $1.8B this year. Same report notes 30–40% of Alibaba International merchants are solo-run.
- **Tencent Penguin (Jul 10)** — China’s first Tencent WorkBuddy OPC (One Person Company) community launched in Chengdu Hi-Tech Zone, co-founded by Tencent Cloud, built on the WorkBuddy AI workbench with a “platform support + community co-creation” growth path for solo founders.
- **Tencent Penguin (Jul 15)** — Beike Finance annual consumer conference in Beijing debuted a “Young Founders Open Mic”; reporters toured multiple OPC industrial parks, framing OPC as moving from concept to mainstream practice for young founders.
- **Tencent Penguin (Jul 15)** — “Dialogue with Super Individuals”: a 3-person AI film studio runs the OPC model via a modular AIGC pipeline (ideation → generation → post → delivery) using low-code tools and external collaboration; flags compute cost, tool-chain cost and stability as real constraints.
- **CSDN (Jul 12)** — OpenCSG shipped CSGClaw + CSGHub-lite, a lightweight stack to “spin up your own AI team in one click”: a Manager agent decomposes a goal and creates Worker agents in isolated sandboxes, targeting local-compute solo operators.
- **CSDN (Jul 13)** — “One-person company + AI agent legion” narrative: a solo dev orchestrates 17 function-specific agents (ideation, coding, social ops, finance) as a 24/7 team, handling only strategy and key decisions behind a multi-million-RMB revenue business.
- **CSDN (Jul 13, SJTU talk)** — Three hard rules for OPC: AI lowers the barrier but raises competitive density (win a narrow niche or die); great product still needs distribution, so personal brand is infrastructure; every workflow step must be evaluated for “can this be AI-ified.”
- **CSDN (Jul 14)** — “Super individual” defined: cross-domain, scalable output at near-zero marginal cost; one decision-maker plus several agents can deliver large projects with 90%+ of returns flowing to the core operator.
- **Y Combinator (Jul 15)** — YC Fall 2026 applications open (deadline Jul 27), $500k per selected startup; continues to draw solo / lean AI founding teams. GitHub’s `ycombinator` topic now lists 139 public repos, heavy on LLM eval and observability tooling.
- **Growitfor.me / IndieMakerList (Jul 15)** — Western indie ecosystem stays active: Growitfor.me pitches AI + prebuilt tools to build and scale a one-person business; John Rush’s daily-updated Indie Maker List catalogs bootstrapped SaaS/directory/app founders.
- **SourceForge (Jul 14)** — Tooling for solo operators expands: Skyvern automates browser workflows via CV + NLP (handles logins, CAPTCHAs, 2FA); LumaBrowser ships a programmable browser for AI agents with REST API, built-in MCP server, and CDP network interception.
- **CCID/Penguin (Jun 30)** — China’s enterprise AI-agent market projected at ¥13.53B in 2026, up 70%+ YoY, shifting from point-tool buying to full-workflow agent systems — deepening the substrate solo founders build on.
