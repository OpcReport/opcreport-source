China’s Ministry of Science and Technology, along with three other government bodies, has inaugurated an AI OPC track within the national AIGC multimodal competition — the first time a state-level platform has opened an independent category for one-person companies. The organizer, Jinshang AI OPC Incubation Platform, simultaneously launched the “Million AI OPC Entrepreneurship Incubation Project,” targeting 1 million AI one-person companies within 10 years, expected to drive employment for 3 million people.
