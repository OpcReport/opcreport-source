# WAIC and OpenCSG Launch First Global Challenge for Solo AI Founders

The most notable development this cycle: World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) partnered with OpenCSG to launch the “OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge” — described as the world’s first top-tier incubator built specifically for super-individuals and AI founders. Backing data cited in the announcement: over one-third of new companies are now solo-founded, a share that has jumped 53% in six years.

- **WAIC × OpenCSG OPC Challenge** (CSDN/OpenCSG, 2026-07-14): Global-first incubation platform for solo AI founders; cites >1/3 of new firms are single-founder, up 53% over six years.
- **“2026 One-Person Company Survival Guide”** (CSDN, 2026-06-13): Advocates a 90-day cash-flow path — validate a paid problem first, then product; delegate roles to AI “digital employees.”

- **Indie dev: v0 → Cursor in 48h** (CSDN, 2026-05-22): Intent-driven dev lets one person ship a SaaS from ideation to launch in 48 hours.
- **Zhima OPC toolbox** (QQ/企鹅号, 2026-05-27): Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Zhejiang, Jiangsu rolling out free desks, subsidies, compute vouchers for OPCs.
