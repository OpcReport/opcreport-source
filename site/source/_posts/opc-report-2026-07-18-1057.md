China’s MIIT and six other ministries issued a joint directive in June 2026 to “accelerate cultivation of AI one-person companies (AIOPC),” with over 20 cities already launching dedicated OPC support policies — the model has moved from individual experiment to national strategy.

- **工信部等七部门 / 企鹅号** — 2026-07-06: Joint directive explicitly names AIOPC as a priority; 20+ Chinese cities now offer OPC-specific incentives; Shanghai, Jiangsu and Guangdong fold OPC into their 15th Five-Year plans.
- **CSDN / OpenCSG** — 2026-07-14: OPC Pioneer Challenge recap cites Sam Altman’s prediction that “one person could build a $1B unicorn in the AI era”; over one-third of new companies are now solo-founded, up 53% in six years.
- **CSDN / OPC中国** — 2026-07-16: Community framework distills OPC to “1 person + 100 agents = 1 company,” with a seven-capability model (strategy, content, IP, brand, product, tech, operations).
- **新华网** — 2026-07-07: Global Digital Economy Conference hosts a dedicated “OPC Co-creation Festival”; Beijing founder Liu Yefei runs a startup with five “AI employees” spanning code, cloud deploy and research.
- **CSDN** — 2026-07-13: Baidu Miaoda 3.0 (first enterprise-grade no-code AI dev platform in China) has served 20M+ users and spawned 1M commercial apps; Alibaba Meoo ships minute-level full-stack generation. A team of 4 no-code managers built an aged-care system in 7 days and won a 10M-yuan order.
- **企鹅号** — 2026-06-27: Chengdu visual-design tool Seede AI (an OPC practitioner) closes a $1M seed round; 300K+ registered users, 10K+ paying, with its own rendering engine bypassing mainstream text-to-image routes.
- **CSDN** — 2026-07-11: 2026 Claude Code hackathon winners were all domain experts, not programmers — a building-permit accelerator (injury lawyer), a cardiac diagnostic aid (interventionist cardiologist) — confirming vertical SaaS as the solo-founder sweet spot.
- **机械工业出版社 / 当当** — 2026-06-23: “OPC in the OpenClaw Era: Methodology from 0 to 1” published, a systematic playbook for super-individuals.
- **CSDN** — 2026-07-12: Six OPC monetization paths mapped — custom agents for SMEs, subscription agent products, AI content, knowledge/IP, tool reselling, e-commerce fulfillment.
- **Ayalr** — 2026-07-05: Solopreneur-focused monitoring tools built “from real dreams, real experience” — systems that watch your one-person stack while you sleep.
- **Indie Hackers** — 2026-07-08: SaasOffers partners with Deel to save founders up to $1,500 on global hiring; a Top-50 Product Hunt launch with zero signups fuels solo-maker launch-strategy debate.
- **Directual** — 2026-06-24: No-code AI-agent platform reports 30,000+ entrepreneur users; full stack (DB, vector search, API, UI) lets solo founders hit MVP fast, code-free.
- **Shorthand AI** — 2026-07-14: “Best AI Tools for Solopreneurs in 2026” tested in a live one-person business under $400/month; plus 7 no-code marketing workflows anyone can build.
- **GitHub / MiniMax** — 2026-07-11: Open-source MMX-CLI gives AI agents full-modal CLI access (text/image/video/voice/music) in two lines, no MCP server — agents move from “chat” to “act.”
- **GitHub / Windrecorder** — 2026-07-16: 563-commit open-source “screen memory search” app (a Rewind/Copilot Recall alternative) lets solo builders search everything on screen by window, text or idle time.
