# One-Third of New Startups Are Solo-Founded as AI Cuts Team Costs 72x

OpenCSG data shows over one-third of new companies are now founded by solo founders — up 53% in six years — while China’s HACR benchmark puts the AI labor-substitution leverage at roughly 72:1, turning “one person plus AI” from slogan into a measurable operating model.

- **OpenCSG / CSDN** (2026-07-14): OPC Pioneer Challenge analysis — over 1/3 of new companies are solo-founded, a 53% jump in six years; core logic framed as “Carbon intelligence (human) + Silicon execution (AI) = complete company.”
- **Honghub / CSDN** (2026-04-13): “2026 One-Person Company Insight Report” introduces HACR (Human-AI Cost Ratio); China 2026 baseline shows every ¥1 of AI spend substitutes ~¥72 of developer labor.
- **Sohu Tech** (2026-05-31): Lovable hit $100M ARR in 8 months at a $6.6B valuation; Israel’s Base44 (8 people, 90% AI-generated code) reached $1.5M ARR in 3 weeks and was acquired for $80M; Suzhou Huazong produced 2,600 AI anime storyboards in 28 days.
- **TechCrunch** (2026-01-26): Industrial AI startup CVector raised $5M for its “brain and nervous system” for heavy industry, now proving ROI to customers and investors.
- **Google Cloud — AI Agent Trends 2026** (2026-05-05): 88% of Agentic AI early adopters already see positive ROI in at least one use case; shift from instruction-driven to intent-driven computing; A2A and MCP pushed as ecosystem standards.
- **Baidu Create / CSDN** (2026-05 launch, reported 2026-07-13): MiaoDa 3.0 no-code AI dev platform uses conversational generation plus a dozen specialized agents; passed 20M users and 1M commercial apps by May 2026.
- **djangokatya.com** (2026-07-17): New tool hub for solo founders & solo devs — utilities for checking vibecoded pages, cleaning browser-only content, auditing hard-to-inspect site parts.
- **GitHub — teze/one-person-company** (2026-06-27): Community repo curating OPC AI tool stacks with live benchmarks (v0.dev, Lovable, bolt.new, Replit) for one-prompt site/App generation.
- **CSDN** (2026-07-13): China’s OPC individual-entrepreneur count now exceeds 12M across Zhongguancun, Wuhan Optics Valley, Shanghai Lin-gang; OPC carries independent legal-person status.
- **Cnblogs — AI Coding benchmark** (2026-04-17): 2026 CLI accuracy — Claude Code 80.9% (first-pass ~95%), Codex CLI 77.3%, Cursor ~73%, Gemini CLI ~65%; pricing free to $20/mo.
- **QQ / YC 2026 readout** (2026-02-26): Y Combinator’s 2026 batch leans hard into AI applications; government AI tooling flagged as an early-starting niche in China.
- **Tencent / Zhima Credit** (2026-05-27): Zhima Enterprise Credit launched an OPC toolbox for one-person companies; Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Zhejiang, Jiangsu rolled out free desks, subsidies, and compute vouchers.
