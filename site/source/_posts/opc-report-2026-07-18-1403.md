WAIC 2026, opening July 17 in Shanghai, launched the world’s first dedicated OPC (One-Person Company) exhibition zone with 180 companies, and the OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge finale is playing out there this month.

- **Tencent / Penguin (企鹅号)** — 2026-07-07: WAIC 2026 (July 17–20, Shanghai) debuts a dedicated “One-Person Company” exhibition zone with 180 companies; new OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge and youth investor leaderboard added. 1,100+ companies and 3,000+ products on show, 300+ AI products launching globally for the first time.
- **Penguin (企鹅号)** — 2026-07-17: WAIC 2026 opens under the theme “Intelligent Partners, Creating the Future.” ~60 humanoid robots deployed at scale for the first time at a major expo for on-site guidance and Q&A. The OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge reaches its WAIC finals and awards this month.
- **OpenCSG / CNBlogs** — 2026-03-23: The OPC Independent Pioneer Challenge runs 10 regional tracks (Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, etc.) with a three-round format (online prelims → city semis → WAIC finals); city semis concluded in May. OpenCSG’s CSGHub aggregates 200,000+ AI models.
- **CSDN Blog** — 2026-06-26: Citing Carta data, solo-founded companies rose from 23.7% of new ventures in 2019 to 36.3% in H1 2025 — a 50%+ jump in six years. Sam Altman’s “one person building a $1B unicorn” prediction is repeatedly invoked as OPC becomes 2026’s hottest model.
- **CSDN Blog** — 2026-07-15: A 2026 OPC panorama guide states the concept was formally introduced at the Suzhou “AI OPC Conference” on 2025-11-11; 20+ Chinese cities have launched dedicated support policies, with the OPC market projected to exceed 800 billion yuan (≈8000亿).
- **Penguin (企鹅号)** — 2026-03-19: Jinan launched China’s first city-level “Digital-Eco OPC Community” in Feb 2026 with ten support measures; 260+ one-person-company projects have applied, each running full loops via multiple AI agents.
- **Sohu** — 2026-06-04: The 2026 Shanghai Super-Individual (OPC) Economy Conference (June 2–3, Lin-gang) surfaced three signals — extreme vertical focus, strong collaboration/ecosystem building, and near-zero-cost startup environments.
- **CSDN Blog** — 2026-07-06: A post reviewing 100+ OPC cases finds three profitable profiles — tech-maestros (Tsinghua PhD’s smart wristband raised 10M+), niche deep-divers (independent 3D game), and service OPCs (AI cut WeChat copywriting from 2–3h to <1h, hitting 2-month income above prior salary).
- **CSDN Blog** — 2026-07-14: Claims Claude Code reached 100% self-written code in May 2026 with 250% output growth, shifting devs to “write prompts, review architecture.” Domestic tool TRAE passed 6M registered users at 98% code-gen accuracy.
- **GitHub** — 2026-03-14: Solo Founders Program (SFP), a 3-month San Francisco accelerator, offers $100K, housing and office space for cohorts of 6. Its “State of Solo Founding” report (with Carta) finds solo founders now start one-third of all new U.S. companies.
- **GitHub** — 2026-06-09: awesome-founder-communities, a curated list for indie makers and solopreneurs, adds Indie Hackers, On Deck, WIP, Makerlog, Launch Club and more.
- **GitHub / CSDN** — 2026-05-25: solopreneur-ai, an open-source one-person-company framework, installs via pip and scaffolds AGENTS.md / SOUL.md / USER.md / MEMORY.md, running multi-LLM “read → analyze → write” loops.
- **Bika.ai** — 2026-07-17: Launches an “AI Organizer for One-Person Company” with multi-agent collaboration and templates (AI Programmer, X/Twitter Manager, expense tracking, investor deal flow).
- **soloist.ai / djangokatya / toollist.ai** — 2026-07-17: A wave of solo-founder tooling ships — Solo (free AI website builder + custom domain), djangokatya’s 5-stage research→build→audit→track→monetize toolkit, and toollist.ai logging 78 AI tools launched that day.
- **f3fundit.com** — 2026-07-08: Publishes deep dives for AI solopreneurs on LangChain vs CrewAI vs custom agents (deployability, cost, real autonomy) and a Model Context Protocol explainer for interoperable agent stacks.
