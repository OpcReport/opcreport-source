## ⚠️ Risk Factors

- **Open ≠ free.** Self-hosting weights means GPU ops; the “free model” hides VRAM, latency, and reliability costs. Without ops chops, the API money you save leaks back into debugging.
- **Voice compliance & reputation.** Voice cloning, consent, and deepfake rules are tightening. Ship voice with clear consent and labeling or step into a legal minefield.
- **Hidden cost of routing.** Shifting load to open models saves money but adds quality/stability variance; agent loops can still run away and burn cash.
- **Single-platform lock-in.** The whole chain sitting on Replit / Claude / ElevenLabs’s terms — one price hike, ban, or policy change (see Replit’s 4-month App Store block) and you have zero buffer.
