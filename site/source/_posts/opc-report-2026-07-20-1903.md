## Risk Factors

- **Routing complexity:** Mixing models fragments versions / formats / timeouts; one unhandled fallback and the whole pipeline breaks — get one model running solid before you route.
- **Open-weight compute trap:** Self-hosting is “free” until the GPU instance bill and ops land; at scale it can cost more than the API — compute TCO before you commit.
- **Vendor price rebound:** Prices halved today can rise tomorrow; make the routing layer swappable so your product isn’t welded to one API.
- **Benchmark inflation:** A high leaderboard score ≠ good on your scenario; Kimi / MiniMax are strong on long context but weaker on instruction-following — test on a real task before launch.
