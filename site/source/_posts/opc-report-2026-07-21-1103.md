## Risk Factors

- **Early-protocol risk.** MCP 7/28 is still a candidate spec; interfaces may shift. Locking to a single client too early is a trap.
- **Payments compliance minefield.** Agentic payments touch authorization boundaries, fund segregation, and KYC. Riding the regulatory blank space while tools like Stripe are still evolving is dangerous.
- **Commoditization.** “Yet another connector / billing wrapper” has a low bar and a short first-mover window; without a vertical moat it gets cloned fast.
- **Security surface.** Self-hosted MCP servers and agents holding keys or email identities are new attack surfaces — treat them as production secrets from day one.
