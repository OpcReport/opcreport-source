### 2. The “agent harness” is becoming its own product

As agents get autonomous enough to touch production systems, a new layer is forming — the harness that safely controls them. The evidence is concrete:

- **The framing is crystallizing.** “Harness engineering” — designing the system that lets autonomous agents run unsupervised without breaking things — is the term of the moment. The opportunity is to productize the harness for a specific workflow.

- **The pattern:** the durable solo-business is an agent wrapped around a data source competitors can’t easily replicate. Own the connector, own the moat.

## Risk Factors

- **Voice/support agents are now crowded.** Dialpad, Intercom, Vapi, Retell and a long tail are all here. Differentiate by vertical depth and owned data, not by another generic bot.
- **Agent autonomy on production is a breach risk.** The Indie Hackers builder’s own agent exposed data; a Meta internal agent reportedly leaked data for ~2 hours. Sandbox everything and require human approval on destructive or external actions.
- **Voice cost scales with minutes.** At volume, per-minute inference eats margin fast if you don’t meter and cap usage per client.
- **Voice vendor lock-in.** ElevenLabs/Vapi pricing can move; keep a swap path (LiteLLM / your own MCP) so a price hike doesn’t sink your margin.
