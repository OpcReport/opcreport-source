## Risk Factors

> **Read before you build:** the window is open, but it rewards operators, not wrappers.

- **Agent turf wars:** multiple agents on one task conflict (Anthropic’s live test) — bad orchestration is messier and more expensive than a single agent.
- **Spend black box:** frontier models got cheaper but usage runs away; without CodeBurn-style monitoring, the month-end bill bites.
- **Commoditization:** voice agents and agent middleware are launching in packs; a thin “VAPI wrapper” or “MCP wrapper” won’t retain.
- **Compliance:** agents that move money or touch customer data (finance agents, on-prem medical inference) trigger KYC / data rules that solo devs routinely underestimate.

## Action Recommendations

**If you’re at 0→1:**

- Pick one of directions ①②③, ship a 3–5 day MVP, validate on Product Hunt / Indie Hackers — don’t write the full SaaS first.
- Split your inference budget: heavy tasks on the now-cheaper GPT-5.6 / Claude, light tasks on local Ollama — get unit cost down first.
- Wire in spend monitoring from day one so “spent how much, produced what” stays visible.

**If you’re at 1→10:**

- Break internal draining functions (finance, ops, support) into “agent-as-coworker”; prefer a Blaze Money-style conversational interface over building your own.
- For multi-agent work, add a control plane (shared space + task locks) to avoid the Anthropic-style infighting.
- Run compute on elastic clusters like Together AI × YC; keep flexibility, don’t pre-buy two years of capacity for peak load.
