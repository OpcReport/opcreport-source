## 💡 Build Directions (High-Priority, Hands-On)

Each direction below names **what to build**, the **exact tool stack**, and a **cost/cycle estimate**.

### Direction A — Vertical micro-SaaS built entirely by agents

- **What:** A small, paid web app for one niche you know (e.g., intake + scheduling for local service businesses, or a compliance checklist tool for a regulated trade).
- **Stack:** **Claude Code** (or Cursor) to scaffold → **Supabase** (Postgres + auth + storage) → **Vercel** (deploy) → **Stripe** (billing).
- **Cost / cycle:** ~$30–80/mo; **MVP in 1–2 weeks**. Charge $20–50/mo from the first real user.

### Direction B — Sell an internal agent to one underserved SMB vertical

- **What:** A “digital employee” that does one painful back-office job (invoice matching, lead qualification, support triage) for a specific industry.
- **Stack:** **n8n** (self-host free) or **Dify** for the workflow → **Claude/GPT API** for reasoning → **Retool or Streamlit** for a lightweight UI → connect to the client’s email/CRM.
- **Cost / cycle:** $0–40/mo to prototype; **2–3 weeks to first paid pilot**. Pricing: monthly retainer $300–1,500.

### Direction C — Autonomous research / newsletter / content engine for a niche

- **What:** A recurring, AI-generated briefing for a tight audience (a trade, a stock sector, a local market) distributed via email or a site.
- **Stack:** **Perplexity API** or **Claude** for research → **Make.com / n8n** to orchestrate → **Substack or your own domain** as the endpoint → **Stripe** for paid tiers.
- **Cost / cycle:** ~$20–60/mo; live in **days**. Monetize via subscriptions or sponsorships.

### Direction D — Managed agent for a regulated or high-friction vertical

- **What:** An agent that handles a sensitive workflow where trust matters (legal intake, real-estate docs, insurance follow-ups) behind a human-in-the-loop approval step.
- **Stack:** **LangGraph** (stateful agent) + hosted vector DB (**Pinecone** free tier) + **WhatsApp/Slack** as the interface + **Claude/GPT** for reasoning.
- **Cost / cycle:** ~$0–50/mo to start; **3–4 weeks** to a defensible pilot. The moat is the compliance wrapper, not the model.

### Direction E — Browser/desktop automation service for ops teams

- **What:** A service that runs repetitive clicks-and-forms tasks on sites with no API, for agencies and ops teams.
- **Stack:** **Anthropic Computer Use** or an open-source agent harness + a sandboxed runner + a simple job queue UI.
- **Cost / cycle:** low prototype cost; sell as per-task or monthly. **Caveat:** reliability on UI changes is the real work — price the maintenance, not just the demo.

## ⚠️ Risk Factors

- **Homogenization:** Agent-builder templates make every MVP look identical. Differentiation now lives entirely in **distribution and proprietary domain data**, not in the tech.
- **Cost creep / lock-in:** Per-token API billing bites — heavy **Claude Code** users report **$100–200/mo** in API usage, which can erase margin on thin SaaS. Model the worst-case bill before you price.
- **Compliance & liability:** Autonomous agents that touch customer data or money create new legal exposure; “agent payments” (prepaid cards for agents) is still unregulated. Keep a human approval step where money moves.
- **Production reliability:** GUI/autonomous agents that “operate outside chat” break on UI changes. The moat is uptime and eval harnesses, not the demo reel.
