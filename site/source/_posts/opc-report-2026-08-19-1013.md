## Risk Factors

- **Commoditization pressure:** Once terminal coding is free, “build a SaaS” is no longer a moat. The edge returns to domain insight and proprietary data; thin wrappers get killed overnight.
- **Landing traps:** Agents fail most at the boundary between clear SOPs and fuzzy demand — when the ask is vague, the agent confidently runs off and ships work that needs human cleanup.
- **Permissions & compliance:** Letting an agent touch payments/databases/cloud is double-edged; without a trust-boundary runtime, one bad call can cause real financial loss or data leakage.
- **Compute & token cost:** Autonomous coding “burns tokens” by default — Garry Tan publicly advised “turn the intensity to max”; without cost control, inference fees eat the margin.
