## ⚠️ Risk Factors

- **Homogenization.** The Claude Code periphery is already crowded (multiple launches on Aug 26 alone). Validate a differentiated gap before committing; don’t ship another generic MCP wrapper.
- **Verification debt (hard to land).** Unreviewed agent output is an unsecured loan against future accuracy. Assign an owner + error-rate threshold to every automated output and sample regularly — otherwise a wrong answer sits in customer chats and a board deck for 11 weeks.
- **Compliance & data.** Observability tools read real conversations. Enterprise buyers require data exclusion and self-hosting options, or you won’t get in the door.
- **Price-rebound risk.** Today’s low prices are often “introductory” (origami.sa explicitly warns they may rise). The moat isn’t “cheap now” — it’s the architecture of route-by-task + caching.
