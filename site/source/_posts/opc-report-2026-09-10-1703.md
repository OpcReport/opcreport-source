## Market Trends

### Voice agents are the easiest AI vertical to cold-start

For two years “AI that makes phone calls” was either a toy or an enterprise contact-center retrofit. In 2026 the inflection arrived: human-grade neural voices, real-time interruption handling, and per-minute pricing let a solo operator deliver “answer the phone, book the appointment, triage the lead” as a production system. Retell AI reached ~$50M ARR on five people, powering 50M+ calls/month, profitable in year one. Phonely closed a $16M Series A (Base10, $100M+ valuation) with customers co-investing — one client booked $10M in insurance in four months and replaced 350 human agents in a month. The angle that fits a solo builder best is **white-label resale**: platforms like Ringlyn hand you the whole stack (voice + telephony + billing) under your brand; you sell and configure. Setup fees $500–2,500, monthly retainers $300–1,500, 60–85% margin. Samora (YC W26) and Callab AI (on-prem PBX, 58% of the $400B call-center market still on legacy systems) show the vertical and enterprise edges are wide open.

## Build Directions

Each direction is shippable by one person in weeks, with a concrete stack and cost/cycle.

### 1. Vertical voice agent (white-label or niche)

- **What:** Pick one high-frequency call scenario — dental booking, real-estate lead qual, post-sale follow-up, collections reminders. Sell it as an “AI receptionist,” or resell white-label to local businesses.
- **Stack:** `Vapi` / `Retell AI` / `Bland` (voice base, ~$0.05–0.15/min + $50–200/mo) + a simple RAG knowledge base from your industry SOPs + `GoHighLevel` / `Make` to orchestrate CRM and follow-up.
- **Cost / cycle:** Live in 1–2 weeks. White-label resale: $500–2,500 setup + $300–1,500/mo retainer, 60–85% margin. **Watch compliance:** call recording, TCPA/TRAI/DND consent and retention vary by region.

### 2. AI-native micro-SaaS for a boring B2B workflow

- **What:** Productize an expensive-but-dull manual process (support triage, contract first-pass, recruiter outreach, e-commerce product research). Proof: Leadmore AI (AI marketing) $30K MRR, Sleek (AI design) $10K MRR in 6 weeks, Launch Fast $30K MRR.
- **Stack:** `Claude Code` ($20–100/mo) or `Cursor` to build + `Lovable` / `Bolt` / `Replit` Pro ($20–25/mo) for UI + `Supabase` (free–$25/mo) for DB/auth + `Vercel` / `Cloudflare` (free–$20) to deploy + `Stripe` to bill.
- **Cost / cycle:** MVP in days to 2 weeks. Price on value (usage/credits), not cost.

### 3. Treat LLM answer engines as your primary distribution channel

- **What:** Publish structured pages / small tools that directly answer the questions your buyers ask ChatGPT. LLM-referred signups convert at 1.66% vs 0.15% for search (11×), and CAC approaches zero. A solo dev already reached $5K MRR this way.
- **Stack:** `Next.js` + structured Q&A / comparison content + `schema markup` + `Vercel`; mine “people also ask” with `Ahrefs` / `AlsoAsked`.
- **Cost / cycle:** Consistent publishing; citations and conversions in 2–3 months; almost no paid acquisition.

### 4. Vertical knowledge-work agent on managed orchestration

- **What:** Contract review, support triage, research summarization — judgment-heavy work using managed orchestration + your proprietary data source + human escalation. ~$180/mo inference for 10K tasks (down from $800 in 2024).
- **Stack:** `Claude Agent SDK` or `Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0` (native MCP + A2A) + a proprietary corpus/data warehouse + a human-in-the-loop switch.
- **Cost / cycle:** Usable build in 2–4 weeks. Moat = proprietary data loop + compliance, not the model.

### 5. Niche outbound / GTM agent

- **What:** Automated lead-finding + personalized outreach + follow-up for one seller type. Proof: Lancer (Upwork lead gen) $10K MRR in 60 days; Meerkats.ai (GTM orchestration) $3K MRR in 4 weeks.
- **Stack:** `Apollo` / `Instantly` (leads + sending) + `Clay` (enrichment) + an LLM agent for copy + `n8n` to orchestrate.
- **Cost / cycle:** 1–2 weeks. Charge per result (booked meetings / qualified leads) — more convincing than per-seat.

## Risk Factors

- **Commoditization kills value.** Thin API-wrapper agents are now nearly worthless as acquisition targets and face brutal price wars — defensibility must come from proprietary workflow data + distribution, not the model.
- **Distribution is the real bottleneck.** Freemius data: 70% of founders earn under $1K MRR, only 18% reach the $1K–5K zone. Building is easy; getting paid is hard.
- **Voice compliance landmines.** Call recording, consent, and TCPA/TRAI/DND rules differ by region — one misstep can shut a voice business down overnight.
- **Inference cost volatility.** API prices fell 60% in two years but can swing back; a usage-priced AI product with thin margins is exposed to provider hikes.
- **Platform lock-in.** Claude Agent SDK is Claude-only; Microsoft AF leans Azure. Orchestration choices now create long-term dependencies.
