## Risk Factors

- **Homogenization:** MCP servers and “agent wrappers” can be cloned in a few lines. Without proprietary data or distribution, you’re a commodity.
- **Reliability trap:** Demos lie — 63% of complex multi-step tasks fail in production. Shipping before measuring on *your* workload burns customer trust.
- **Compliance:** The EU AI Act’s general application began August 2, 2026. Agent memory holding personal data needs provenance, retention, and deletion governance — or you’re exposed.
- **Dependency & version risk:** Betting on one model vendor or one local-hardware spec is fragile; model and protocol churn is fast (the new MCP version already outpaces clients). Locking either end in gets you left behind.
