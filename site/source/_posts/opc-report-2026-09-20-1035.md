## Build Directions (the actionable part)

### Direction 1 — Vertical AI agent for one painful workflow

**What:** Pick a single, repetitive, high-volume operational job and ship an agent that does most of it with a human approval gate. Support triage, sales-lead qualification, recruiting screeners, and invoice matching are all proven. ProductBridge’s model (draft, you approve for the first month) is the right safety pattern.  
**Stack:** Cursor + Claude Code for build · Supabase (Postgres + auth + edge functions) · n8n or Make for the trigger/glue · Stripe for billing if SaaS · deploy on Vercel.  
**Cost / cycle:** ~$150–400/mo once you have paying users; MVP in 3–7 days. The moat is the workflow’s approval logic and the data you accumulate, not the model.

### Direction 2 — GEO / AI-search-visibility SaaS or consultancy

**What:** A tool or service that tells a business “are AI answers citing you, and for which prompts?” Start as a done-with-you audit, then productize into a monitoring SaaS (run target prompts across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini on a schedule, show citation share vs competitors). Visiby proved demand; the field is wide open below enterprise pricing.  
**Stack:** Next.js + Supabase · call model APIs (or the LLM tracking APIs) to run prompts · Resend for reports · Stripe · deploy on Vercel/Cloudflare.  
**Cost / cycle:** ~$50–200/mo in API + infra; first audit product in 1–2 weeks. Biggest risk is measurement credibility — use a real LLM-tracking source, not vibes.

### Direction 3 — “Bring the agent to where work happens” connector

**What:** The agent-deployment layer is fragmented. Switch showed the appetite: a single integration that puts any agent into Slack/Teams/Discord with shared context. A solo builder can ship a narrower version — e.g., an MCP connector that links a specific SaaS (your CRM, your inbox) to any coding or ops agent. Catenary and OpenMarket suggest the same hunger for agent-composable plumbing.

**Cost / cycle:** near-zero infra to start (Cloudflare free tier); 1–2 weeks to a usable connector. Distribution is the hard part — launch on Product Hunt and the relevant agent communities, not SEO.

## Risk Factors

- **The hidden runtime-cost cliff.** Credit-based builders (Lovable, Base44) understate total cost of ownership; for a live product, real spend is often **2–3× the headline subscription** once runtime and AI calls bill on top. Prototype on them; don’t mistake the prototype for production economics.
- **Agents write auth, validation, and billing confidently and wrongly.** Unattended agents with Stripe or CRM credentials, or AI-generated authorization/secret-handling code, fail *silently*. Keep a human approval gate and logging until the workflow earns trust.
- **Context-window pain is the real bottleneck.** ~62% of developers cite context (not model accuracy) as their top frustration — agents forget across files and overwrite their own changes. Don’t bet a product on an agent that can’t hold a project in its head.
- **AI-search cannibalization hits your own distribution.** If your growth plan is “rank on Google,” AI Overviews now intercept nearly half of searches before the click. Build GEO into every content page from day one, or you’ll be invisible to the buyers who already asked an AI.
