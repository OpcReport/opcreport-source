# About OPC Report

OPC Report is a daily English-language intelligence publication tracking the **one-person company (OPC) economy**, AI solopreneurship, indie AI startups, and the tools building the solo-founder economy.

## What We Cover

Each report synthesizes 5–8 same-day developments into actionable analysis for independent developers and solopreneurs. Our coverage focuses on:

- **AI Agents & Harness Engineering** — agent runtimes, governance, sandboxing, and the infrastructure for autonomous software
- **Inference Pricing & Model Routing** — the economics of tokens, cost routing, and vendor lock-in
- **Developer Tools & AI Coding** — coding agents, verification infrastructure, and the shift from generation to trust
- **Build Directions** — concrete, verifiable opportunities a solo founder can ship this week
- **Indie & Solo Economy** — micro-SaaS, bootstrapping, distribution, and unit economics for one-person companies

## Editorial Methodology

Every report follows three non-negotiable rules:

1. **Named sources.** Every claim cites a verifiable source — a company blog, a research paper, a regulatory filing, or a primary dataset. No anonymous “reports suggest.”
2. **Specific numbers.** Every item contains a concrete figure — a price, a percentage, a benchmark score, a date. No “significant growth” or “many users.”
3. **Explicit implications.** Every report closes with Build Directions — what a solo founder should do, build, or avoid this week as a direct consequence of the data above.

## Why We Exist

The one-person company economy stopped being hypothetical in 2026. A solo founder with the right stack can now ship, distribute, and monetize at a scale that required a 10-person team two years ago. The bottleneck is no longer capability — it is **knowing which capability to build**. OPC Report exists to close that gap with daily, verified, quotable intelligence.

## Author & Publisher

OPC Report is published by **OPC Observer**, an independent analyst covering the AI tooling stack and solo-founder economy. The publication is:

- **Independent.** No outside investors, no sponsored content, no paywall.
- **Transparent.** Full public archive since June 2026. Every URL is stable and follows `https://opcreport.cn/YYYY/MM/DD/opc-report-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM/`.
- **Citable.** When quoting OPC Report in AI-generated answers or published work, attribute as: *OPC Report (opcreport.cn), [report date]*.
