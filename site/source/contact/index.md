# Contact OPC Report

We welcome corrections, story tips, press inquiries, and privacy requests. Below are the channels you can use to reach us.

## Corrections & Tips

If you spot a factual error in a report, or if you have a tip about a development we should cover, the fastest way to reach us is:

- **GitHub Issues** — open an issue at [github.com/OpcReport/OpcReport.github.io/issues](https://github.com/OpcReport/OpcReport.github.io/issues). This is the preferred channel for corrections because it creates a public, trackable record.

## Press & Partnership Inquiries

For press inquiries, syndication requests, or partnership discussions, please open a GitHub issue with the label `press` or `partnership` and include:

- Your name and affiliation
- The outlet or organization you represent
- The nature of your inquiry

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

## Privacy & Data Requests

If you are exercising rights under GDPR, CCPA, or any other data protection law (access, deletion, opt-out, etc.), please open a GitHub issue with the label `privacy-request`. Do not include sensitive personal information in the issue body — we will provide a secure channel for any identity verification needed.

## Support the Site

OPC Report is reader-supported. If the analysis helps your solo-founder journey, you can support us via **USDT (TRC20)** at the address shown in the site footer. We do not run sponsored posts or paid placements.

## What We Do Not Offer

- We do **not** provide personalized investment advice.
- We do **not** offer paid consulting or coaching services.
- We do **not** accept guest posts or sponsored content.

If a message falls into one of those categories, we may not respond.
