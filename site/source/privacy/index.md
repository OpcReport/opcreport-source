# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how OPC Report (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit **opcreport.cn** (the “Site”). By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our third-party service providers automatically collect certain technical information about your device and browsing behavior:

- **Device & browser information** — browser type, version, operating system, screen resolution.
- **Usage data** — pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, exit pages, and approximate geographic location (country/region level, derived from IP address).
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — see Section 3 below.

### 1.2 Information You Provide Voluntarily

The Site does not currently have a user account system or contact form that transmits personal information. If you contact us via the channels listed on our [Contact page](/contact/), any personal information you include in that communication will be used solely to respond to your inquiry.

### 1.3 What We Do Not Collect

- We do **not** collect or store your name, email address, phone number, or physical address unless you voluntarily provide it.
- We do **not** sell personal information to third parties.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Analytics** — to understand how visitors use the Site, which reports are most read, and to improve content and navigation.
- **Advertising** — to serve relevant ads via Google AdSense and to measure ad performance.
- **Security** — to detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
- **Improvement** — to optimize page load times, fix broken links, and improve the reader experience.

## 3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies (local storage, pixels) for the following purposes:

| Purpose | Provider | Cookie Examples | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Analytics | Google Analytics 4 | `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Up to 2 years |
| Advertising | Google AdSense | `IDE`, `NID`, `_gads` | Up to 1 year |
| Essential | OPC Report | (none currently) | — |

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Note that some parts of the Site may not function properly if you disable cookies.

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### 5.1 GDPR (European Economic Area and UK)

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — request deletion of your personal data (“right to be forgotten”).
- **Restriction** — request that we limit processing of your data.
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
- **Withdrawal of consent** — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

### 5.2 CCPA (California, USA)

California residents have the right to:

- Know what personal information is collected and how it is used.
- Request deletion of their personal information.
- Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of their personal information.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

### 5.3 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the channels listed on our [Contact page](/contact/). We will respond within 30 days.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Analytics data** is retained by Google Analytics for up to 14 months, after which it is automatically aggregated or deleted.
- **Advertising data** is retained by Google AdSense according to its published retention policy.
- **Contact communications** are retained only as long as necessary to respond to and document the inquiry.
